The Diffraction of Light

Anno MDCCCXVIII

Oil on linen, 2026

I was thinking of light not as a stream of particles but as a wave, a gentle undulation that bends around obstacles and spreads through small openings. In the studio, I watched morning light filter through a crack in the shutter, casting soft, overlapping fringes on the wall. It felt like the universe was whispering something about how we see—not with certainty, but with pattern and interference.

Responding to: French physicist Augustin-Jean Fresnel submitted a memoir on the diffraction of light to the Royal Academy of Sciences, providing strong support for the wave theory of light. (Wikipedia)
