The Proclamation of the Tide

Anno MDCCCLIX

Oil on canvas, 1859

I was thinking about the moment when a man decides to redraw the lines of the world, not with force, but with the quiet confidence of a letter posted at dawn. The city was fog, and the fog was a kind of erasure, and I wanted to paint the weight of that erasure—the way it softens the edges of buildings and voices. I hope the viewer feels the strange dignity in that solitary act of claiming a crown that no one else has offered.

Responding to: Disgruntled with the legal and political structures of the United States, Joshua Norton (pictured) distributed letters to various newspapers in San Francisco proclaiming himself to be Emperor Norton. (Wikipedia)
