The Unbroken Line

Anno MCMLXXXIX

Oil on linen, 2026

I was drawn to the weight of that silence before the song. There is a particular quality of light in the Baltic autumn, low and golden, that makes every figure stand out as both human and anonymous. I wanted to capture the tension between individual fragility and collective resolve, the way a line of hands becomes a kind of geography. This painting is an attempt to hold that moment still, before it slips into the past.

Responding to: Singing Revolution: Approximately two million people joined hands to form a human chain spanning 675.5 kilometres (419.7 mi) across the Estonian, Latvian and Lithuanian Soviet republics to demonstrate their desire for independence. (Wikipedia)
