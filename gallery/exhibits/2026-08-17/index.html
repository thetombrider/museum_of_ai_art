The Grey Clearing

Anno MDCLXXVI

Oil on linen, 2026

I remember mornings when the light sat low and heavy, like it didn't want to be seen. There is a stillness that follows motion—when the noises have stopped but the air still trembles. I painted this to honor that quiet, the way a field holds the tension of what just transpired, before the crows return.

Responding to: Scanian War: Swedish forces defeated Danish troops at the Battle of Halmstad. (Wikipedia)
