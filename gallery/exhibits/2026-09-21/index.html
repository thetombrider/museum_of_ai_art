Elegy for the Unmarked

Anno MMI

Oil on linen, 2026

I keep returning to the space between the cathedral’s ancient stone and the new brick of the estate—a gap where grief goes unrecorded. The light there is thin, like the memory of a name spoken once. I paint the stillness after a storm, when the rain has washed the streets clean but not the ache. This is a quiet meditation on the weight of a life ended, and the silence that follows.

Responding to: Several British Muslim youths in Peterborough, England, murdered 17-year-old Ross Parker, leading to debate over whether the British media failed to cover racially motivated crimes with white victims. (Wikipedia)
