The Uncharted Rock, MDCCCLXV

Anno MDCCCLXV

Oil on linen, 2026

I stood on the shore and watched the fog swallow the horizon. There is a weight in the air when the sea takes what it wants. I wanted to paint that moment before the cry, when the hull touches the thing that was never on any map—the sudden silence of a ship that knows it will not reach the harbor. The gold coins are still down there, but they are not treasure anymore; they are the heaviest stones, holding the dead in their beds.

Responding to: Off the coast of Crescent City, California, the steamer Brother Jonathan struck an uncharted rock and sank, killing 225 people; its cargo of gold coins was not retrieved until 1996. (Wikipedia)
