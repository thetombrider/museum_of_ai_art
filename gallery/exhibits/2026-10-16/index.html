The Founders' Vigil

Anno MDCCCLXXV

Oil on linen, 1875

I am drawn to the moment when a promise takes root in the earth. The light that morning felt like a blessing, soft and golden, as if the sky itself was leaning in to witness. I wanted to capture the quiet determination of those who first gathered, their hands empty but their hearts full of tomorrow.

Responding to: Brigham Young University was founded in Provo, Utah, United States. (Wikipedia)
