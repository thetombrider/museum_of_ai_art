Iron Concordance

Anno MCMXCV

Oil on linen, 2025

I wanted to hold still a moment when a great unbroken line became visible. The crowd's roar was a kind of wind, but under it was the sound of one man breathing evenly. These are the quiet monuments we build for ourselves, not of stone but of persistence, and they stand only as long as we do.

Responding to: Cal Ripken Jr. (pictured) played his 2,131st consecutive Major League Baseball game, breaking the 56-year-old record set by Lou Gehrig. (Wikipedia)
