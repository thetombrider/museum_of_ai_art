The Unquiet Earth, MCMXL

Anno MCMXL

Oil on linen, 2026

I paint the stillness after violence, when the air holds its breath. The fields remember what the witnesses cannot speak. My brush moves slowly, as if afraid to disturb the dust. This is a landscape of silence, but not of peace.

Responding to: Hungarian forces massacred at least 150 ethnic Romanians in Ip, Transylvania, following rumors that Romanians were responsible for the deaths of two soldiers. (Wikipedia)
