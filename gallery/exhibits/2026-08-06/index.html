Fault Line Afternoon

Anno MCMLXXIX

Oil on canvas, 1979

I remember the stillness that follows a sudden rupture—not silence, but a kind of held breath. There is a particular quality of light after the ground has shaken, when dust hangs in the air and objects we trusted betray us. I wanted to capture that moment of quiet disbelief, where the world we thought we knew tilts just slightly, and we are left to rearrange our understanding. The painting is a meditation on fragility and the patience of repair.

Responding to: An earthquake struck along the Calaveras Fault near Coyote Lake, California, injuring sixteen people. (Wikipedia)
