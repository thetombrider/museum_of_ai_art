Low Ceiling Over Bayou

Anno MCMLXXXVIII

Oil on linen, 2026

That summer, the heat had settled into everything—the floorboards, the river, the bones. When the sky finally lowered itself, it did so without thunder, without warning, just a slow press of humidity and light. I remember the moment the air changed, how the mosquitoes grew still, and how the water turned the color of old pewter. I painted this to hold that silence before the first drop fell.

Responding to: Tropical Storm Beryl formed over southeastern Louisiana, making it one of the few tropical cyclones to form over land. (Wikipedia)
