The Blue Sun, MCML

Anno MCML

Oil on linen, 1950

I remember the light that autumn. It was not the light of the sun I knew, but something else, a pale blue disc hanging in a sky that seemed to hold its breath. The smoke was invisible, yet it changed everything. I painted the world as it appeared to me then: muted, distant, as if seen through a veil of memory.

Responding to: The "Great Smoke Pall", generated by the Chinchaga fire, the largest recorded fire in North American history, was first recorded in present-day Nunavut and may eventually have circled the entire globe. (Wikipedia)
