The Accord at Scutari

Anno MCMXII

Oil on linen, 1912

When the demands were met, I thought not of victory but of the long silence that follows any truce. These men had held their breath for months, and now the air was still. I wanted to paint that stillness—the moment before relief, when the weight of history shifts but has not yet settled.

Responding to: The Albanian revolt ended when the Ottoman government agreed to meet most of the rebels' demands. (Wikipedia)
