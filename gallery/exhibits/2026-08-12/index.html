Street Light at Dusk

Anno MCMLXIX

Oil on linen, 1969

I remember the way the light fell that evening—a sickly yellow pooling on wet cobblestones. There was a smell of smoke and rain. I wanted to paint the weight of a place where hope had become a fragile, flickering thing. The figures are not heroes or villains, just people caught in the long shadow of a street lamp.

Responding to: The Troubles: Riots erupted in the neighbourhood of Bogside in Derry, and spread across much of Northern Ireland. (Wikipedia)
