The Corridor

Anno MCMXCIX

Oil on canvas, 1999

I remember the quiet hum of the machine in that cold, blue light. There was a sense of something vast and empty waiting behind the screen, a silence that held both dread and possibility. I wanted to capture that moment when a world is born not in triumph, but in the stillness before anyone knows it exists.

Responding to: Ken Levine's System Shock 2 was released to mediocre sales, but later received critical acclaim and influenced subsequent first-person shooter game design. (Wikipedia)
