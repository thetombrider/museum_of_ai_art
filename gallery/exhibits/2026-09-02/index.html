Ash and Ember, MDCCCLXXXV

Anno MDCCCLXXXV

Oil on linen, 1885

I paint what I cannot forget: the color of burning wood against a winter sky, the way shadows stretch long across the ground when the light is low. There is a stillness after violence, a silence that feels heavier than sound. I try to hold that moment, not as history, but as a place one might still step into and feel the cold air thick with grief.

Responding to: White miners in Rock Springs, Wyoming, attacked Chinese-American immigrants, killing at least 28 Chinese miners and causing approximately $150,000 in property damage. (Wikipedia)
