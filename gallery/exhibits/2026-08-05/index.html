The Weight of the Rule

Anno MCMLXXIII

Oil on canvas, 1973

The moment after the final whistle is the quietest. I tried to capture that stillness, the way a gymnasium holds its breath when the games are no longer allowed. The rules are not just lines on a floor; they are the things that hold us together. This painting is about the space between action and consequence, where the only sound is the echo of a bounced ball that will never come.

Responding to: Due to an athletics scandal, the National Collegiate Athletic Association applied the death penalty to the basketball program at the University of Southwestern Louisiana. (Wikipedia)
