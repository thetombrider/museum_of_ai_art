The Stained Carpet, MCMXCI

Anno MCMXCI

Oil on linen, 2026

I keep returning to the moment after the last handshake, when the room is still warm but the laughter has faded. There's a particular quality of light in corridors where so many have passed through—it diffuses memory, softens edges, but never washes away the stain. I paint the carpet because it holds the weight of every step, every gesture, every silence that followed.

Responding to: At the Tailhook Association symposium in Las Vegas, U.S. Navy and Marine Corps aviators were alleged to have sexually assaulted 90 persons. (Wikipedia)
