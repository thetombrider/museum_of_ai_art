The Silence After the Last Bell

Anno MMVII

Oil on canvas, 2007

I paint what remains when the argument is over. The light in that temple yard was heavy, the kind that clings to wet grass and animal breath. I wanted to hold the moment before the door closed—not the conflict, but the quiet dignity of a creature who trusted us. There is a grief in these forms that has no name.

Responding to: After widespread controversy throughout Wales, Shambo, a black Friesian bull that had been adopted by the local Hindu community, was slaughtered due to concerns about bovine tuberculosis. (Wikipedia)
