The Weight of Purple

Anno DLXXXII

Egg tempera and gold leaf on panel, 582

I tried to paint the moment when a man understands that he is no longer just a soldier. The crown is not a reward—it is a door closing behind him. I wanted the gold to feel heavy, like the last light before a storm.

Responding to: Maurice was crowned as Byzantine emperor, succeeding Tiberius II Constantine. (Wikipedia)
