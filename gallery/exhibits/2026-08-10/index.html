The Last Embarkation

Anno MMVII

Oil on linen, 2007

I saw the crowd that day, not as a mob but as a human tide breaking against a granite shore. Their faces held the same light that once fell on ceremonial arrivals, but now it was a farewell light, amber and thick as honey. I wanted to paint the moment when a place ceases to be a landmark and becomes a memory—when the stone itself begins to ache.

Responding to: Amid large protests against the impending demolition of the Queen's Pier, the Hong Kong High Court dismissed legal attempts to preserve the landmark. (Wikipedia)
