The Sultan's Accord, MCCXVIII

Anno MCCXVIII

Oil on linen, 2026

I tried to paint the moment when power becomes tenderness. The air in that room was heavy with dust and the scent of old cedars. I wanted to show a man whose hand, having held a sword, now holds a key, not as a trophy but as a question.

Responding to: Al-Kamil became the fourth sultan of the Ayyubid dynasty of Egypt. (Wikipedia)
