The Weight of Unbroken Light

Anno MDCCLVIII

Oil on linen, 1758

I remember the stillness before the storm, the way the sky held its breath over the fields. The men stood in their ranks, but I saw only the terrible patience of the earth, waiting to be torn. I painted this to hold that moment, the quiet that knows what is coming.

Responding to: Seven Years' War: Prussian forces engaged the Russians at the Battle of Zorndorf in present-day Sarbinowo, Poland. (Wikipedia)
