The Unfinished Parchment

Anno MDCCXCI

Oil on linen, 2026

I found myself haunted by the weight of a single sheet of paper, a declaration that promised so much yet was folded into silence. The ink still glistens, but the hand that wrote it trembles between hope and sacrifice. I wanted to capture that moment when words become a mirror, and the mirror cracks.

Responding to: French playwright Olympe de Gouges published the Declaration of the Rights of Woman and of the Female Citizen, hoping to expose the failures of the French Revolution in the recognition of gender equality. (Wikipedia)
