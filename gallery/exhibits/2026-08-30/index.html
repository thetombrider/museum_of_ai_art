The Chalk Map at the Edge of the Forest

Anno MCMXIV

Oil on linen, 1914

I wanted to paint the stillness before the noise. The maps on the tables, the pins still holding their positions—these are the quiet objects of war. I find myself drawn to the space between decisions and their consequences, where the air itself seems to hold its breath. In that gap, I see the absurd fragility of human ambition.

Responding to: World War I: The Battle of Tannenberg resulted in the almost complete destruction of the Russian 2nd Army by the German 8th Army. (Wikipedia)
