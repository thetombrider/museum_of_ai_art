The Rail at Dusk

Anno MDCCCLXIV

Oil on linen, 1865

I wanted to paint not the noise of battle, but the silence that follows. The Weldon line lay broken like a promise, and I thought of all the hands that would never again touch a rail, a tool, a loved one. The smoke still clings to the trees as if the forest itself cannot let go. This is a piece about the weight of a severed connection, and the earth's quiet witness.

Responding to: American Civil War: At the Battle of Globe Tavern, Union forces attempted to sever the Weldon Railroad during the siege of Petersburg. (Wikipedia)
