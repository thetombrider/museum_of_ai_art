The Vice-Regal Winter

Anno MCMXI

Oil on linen, 1911

He carried the weight of a distant crown into a landscape that did not yet know him. The cold here is different—it does not defer to rank. I wanted to paint the moment when ceremony meets that indifference, and a man becomes a silhouette.

Responding to: Prince Arthur, a son of Queen Victoria, became the first Governor General of Canada of royal descent, as well as the first Prince of Great Britain and Ireland to hold that position. (Wikipedia)
