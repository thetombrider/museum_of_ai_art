Retreat in the August Haze

Anno MDCCLXXVI

Oil on linen, 2026

I've been thinking about the way a landscape can hold memory without announcing it. The smoke from distant cannon fire hangs like fog, and the light is that peculiar amber of late summer that seems to slow everything down. I wanted to paint the moment before the outcome is known, when all that exists is the weight of a decision and the smell of damp grass underneath boot leather.

Responding to: British forces led by William Howe defeated the Continental Army under George Washington at the Battle of Long Island, the largest battle of the American Revolutionary War. (Wikipedia)
