The Long Siege in August Heat

Anno MDLXX

Oil on canvas, 2026

I wanted to capture the weight of a morning that never truly arrived. The dust was still, the light low and amber, as if the sun itself had forgotten to rise. There is a silence that precedes great violence, and I sought to paint that stillness—a moment before the world changes forever.

Responding to: Ottoman–Venetian War: After a 40-day siege, the Cypriot city of Nicosia fell to the Ottomans, leading to a massacre of more than 20,000 citizens. (Wikipedia)
