The Bridge at Dusk

Anno MDCCXCVI

Oil on linen, 2026

I stood on the bank and felt the cold weight of the river. There is a moment before any crossing when the air holds its breath. I tried to paint that silence, the way smoke clings to the stone and the light fails slowly. The men are small against the water, but their shadows are long.

Responding to: War of the First Coalition: General Franz Petrasch's Austrian and Imperial troops stormed the French-held bridgehead over the Rhine river. (Wikipedia)
