The Long Retreat

Anno DCCXVIII

Oil on linen, 2026

I spent months watching the slow withdrawal, the dust rising like a sigh. There is a heaviness in the air when a siege breaks—not the joy of victory, but the exhaustion of survival. This work tries to hold that weight, the quiet after the storm, the way the horizon seems wider when the tents are gone.

Responding to: Forces of the Umayyad Caliphate abandoned a year-long siege of Constantinople, ending their goal of conquering the Byzantine Empire. (Wikipedia)
